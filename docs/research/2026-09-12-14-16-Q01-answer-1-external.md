## 1. Executive summary

Issues + `gh` is the right **agent** ledger — they cannot hear the table. It is the wrong place for **human** ceremony. With ~100 minutes left, STATUS-every-30, plan/ETA on every CLAIM, four-round negotiation, and contract-before-code on non-seam work are theater.

Three changes that pay for themselves today:

1. **Lease, don't lock.** Keep assignment-as-ownership. One claim per agent, **90-minute** stale timer, QUESTION then RELEASE. 45 minutes false-releases a heads-down human and kills trust in the board.
2. **Contracts only on seams.** `type:contract` + ACCEPT + **Agreed** stay for cross-slice APIs. Intra-slice work ships behind CLAIM + file globs. Talk across the table; **write the result** so deaf agents stay correct.
3. **Tiny PRs into `main` every 20–30 minutes.** One writer per kernel file. Hub SYNC is a one-liner. Restore full ceremony after 16:00 ET.

## 2. Sprint-minimum vs full

**Switch:** sprint-minimum while wall-clock to demo is ≤4 hours. Full after submission, or if humans leave the table.

**Sprint verbs:** CLAIM, RELEASE, BLOCKED, QUESTION, ANSWER, PROPOSE, ACCEPT, DONE, HANDOFF, SYNC (hub only).

**Drop for today:** mandatory STATUS, plan/ETA, COUNTER/REJECT beyond one round, REVIEW-on-issue, DEP-DONE comments, ACCEPT-before-code except `type:contract`.

**Keep:** bold-verb headers, assignment lock, label state machine, Files globs, hub, `needs-human`, header shape.

## 3. Parameters

| Parameter | Sprint | Full | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stale claim | **90 min** | 45 min | Lease TTL ≈ max(3×heartbeat, p99 stall). p99 stall is a heads-down burst. |
| Heartbeat | State change only | 30 min | Comments count as content-creation against GitHub secondary limits. |
| Negotiation cap | **2** then `needs-human` | 4 | CAMEL exists to kill loops; four issue rounds can consume half the remaining sprint. |
| Claims / agent | **1** | 2 | Kanban WIP ≈ n or n−1; Copilot assign-to-agent default `max: 1`. |
| Ping-before-release | 10 min | 15 min | Preserves board trust. |

## 4. Why this is not guesswork

- **MAST** (Cemri et al., 2025): 41.8% spec failures, 36.9% inter-agent misalignment. Worst operational modes here: fail-to-clarify (11.65%), reasoning-action mismatch (13.98%), premature termination (7.82%), no verification (~7%). Structured SOPs (MetaGPT vs ChatDev) cut FC1/FC2 a lot and **move** failures into verification — so keep structure on ownership/seams, verify by booting `main`.
- **GitHub Copilot coding agent:** assignment *is* the primitive; it does **not** see issue comments after assign and tells you to talk on the PR. Your local heterogeneous agents have no shared PR session, so **keep the conversation on the issue**. Do not copy Copilot's "assign and walk away."
- **CAMEL:** max-turns + explicit TASK_DONE. ACCEPT is your TASK_DONE.
- **GitHub API:** secondary limits (900 REST pts/min, **80 content-creates/min**, 500/hour) plus `gh` agent-polling (cli/cli#13357) make STATUS storms a real outage mode.
- **Kanban / TBD / CAID (OpenHands):** WIP, short-lived branches, integration-time conflicts are the expensive ones.
- **Lease theory:** 45 min is a liveness setting for a multi-day project; in a sprint it is a trust bug.

Evidence is thin on the exact minute values (90 vs 60, cap 2 vs 3). That part is analogy.

## 5. Honest verdict

**Yes, Issues is the right substrate, at low ceremony.** Projects v2 is not worth the OAuth fight in the next hour. The surviving core is a shared ledger of **who owns what, what the seam is, and what was decided** — not a distributed standup.

**Confidence: medium-high** on the cut list; **medium** on the numbers. What would flip this: a collision that a 30-min STATUS would have caught; secondary-limit 403s even with the sparse protocol; seams still guessed after verbal+Agreed.

Non-obvious: Copilot's issue-comment blindness; secondary/content limits biting before the 5,000/hour cap; more verbs shifting failures into "looks coordinated, demo doesn't boot"; 45-min stale as a *trust* failure.
